AI trust boundaries

The useful work in agentic operations is not the model, it is the boundary. Every sub-agent is deployed against an explicit answer to one question: may it act, or may it only advise? Hallucination guards, action allow-lists and human confirmation gates are designed before deployment, not retro-fitted after an incident.

Acts autonomously
  • Classification and severity proposal on intake
  • Signal correlation and duplicate collapsing
  • Timeline assembly and stakeholder summaries
  • Routing to the owning team and runbook retrieval
Advises only — human confirms
  • Anything that touches production state
  • Customer, partner and regulator-facing communication
  • Declaring, escalating or closing a major incident
  • Root-cause conclusions entering the permanent record
